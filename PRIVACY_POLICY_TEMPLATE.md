# NuScan privacy policy draft

NuScan is a free, offline-first document utility developed by NudroidLabs. Core document operations such as PDF conversion, compression, OCR, signing and password protection are designed to run on the user's device. NuScan app code does not intentionally upload document contents to a NudroidLabs server.

NuScan may use third-party Google services for advertising, privacy consent, document scanning and QR code scanning. Those services may process device or usage information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation. The final published policy must describe the production SDK configuration and the declarations made in Google Play Data safety.

NuScan does not require a paid subscription or Play Billing purchase.

Before publishing this policy, replace this draft with verified production details, a contact method, applicable retention information and any legally required regional disclosures.
