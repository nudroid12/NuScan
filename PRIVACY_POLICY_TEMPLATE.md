# NuScan Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: replace before publication.

NuScan is an offline-first document utility developed by NudroidLabs. Core document operations such as scanning, PDF conversion, compression, OCR, signing and password protection are designed to run on the user's device. NuScan app code does not intentionally upload document contents to a NudroidLabs server.

NuScan may use third-party Google services for advertising, privacy consent, Google Play purchases, document scanning and QR code scanning. Those services may process device or usage information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation. The final published policy must describe the production SDK configuration and the declarations made in Google Play Data safety.

Users can manage advertising privacy choices from the in-app Settings page when the consent platform requires a privacy options entry point.

Before publishing this policy, replace this draft with verified production details, a contact method, applicable retention information and any legally required regional disclosures.
